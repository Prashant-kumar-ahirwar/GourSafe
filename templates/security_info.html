Safety Guidance
Critical
Do not confront suspicious persons. Report them to security immediately.

Daily habits
Always carry your student ID on campus.
Keep your hostel room locked at all times, even when inside.
Walk in well-lit areas at night and use shuttle services whenever available.
In an emergency
Fire: alert others, evacuate immediately, and call 101 or 112.
Memorise at least one emergency number. Do not depend only on the app.
