About the Campus

Spread across ~1,313 acres on Pathariya Hills (Vindhya Range), the campus is one of the largest in central India. Familiarise yourself with the layout — distances between buildings can be significant.

Founded 18 July 1946 · Central University since 15 January 2009.

Safety Guidelines
  • ⚡ Critical
  • Do not confront suspicious persons — report them to security immediately.
  • Use the SOS button on the home page if you cannot speak safely on a call.
  • Daily habits
  • Always carry your student ID on campus.
  • Keep your hostel room locked at all times, even when inside.
  • Do not prop open access-controlled doors for strangers.
  • Walk in well-lit areas at night; use the campus shuttle when available.
  • In an emergency
  • Fire: alert others, evacuate immediately, then call 101 (Fire Service) or 112.
  • Memorise at least one emergency number — don't rely solely on this app.
